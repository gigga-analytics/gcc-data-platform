## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Financial Overview

#### Monthly Revenue Summary
**Location:** Top left panel  
**Update Frequency:** Daily at 09:00 CET  

**Key Metrics:**
- **Total Revenue:** Current month revenue across all business units
- **Net Profit:** Revenue minus expenses for the current month
- **Profit Margin:** Net profit as percentage of total revenue
- **YoY Growth:** Year-over-year revenue growth percentage

**How to Interpret:**
- Green trend arrows indicate positive growth
- Red trend arrows indicate decline requiring attention
- Hover over charts for detailed breakdowns

#### Revenue by Business Unit
**Location:** Center panel  
**Visualization:** Stacked bar chart showing monthly trends  

**Business Units:**
- **Digital Products:** App development, web platforms, digital innovation
- **Consulting:** Strategy consulting, digital transformation
- **Innovation:** R&D projects, emerging technology initiatives

**Key Insights:**
- Identify which business units are driving growth
- Spot seasonal patterns in revenue
- Compare performance across units

### 2. Customer Metrics

#### Customer Acquisition
**Location:** Top right panel  
**Metrics:**
- **New Customers:** Number of new customers this month
- **Customer Growth Rate:** Monthly percentage increase in customer base
- **Pipeline Value:** Total value of opportunities in sales pipeline

#### Customer Health
**Location:** Customer section  
**Metrics:**
- **Active Customers:** Customers with activity in last 90 days
- **At-Risk Customers:** Customers with declining engagement
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Average satisfaction score from recent surveys

**Action Items:**
- Review at-risk customers for retention initiatives
- Analyze satisfaction trends for service improvements
- Track conversion rates from pipeline to closed deals

### 3. Operational Excellence

#### Project Delivery
**Metrics:**

#### Team Utilization
**Metrics:**

### 4. Market Position

#### Digital Presence
**Metrics:**

#### Competitive Analysis
**Metrics:**

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Financial KPIs

#### Revenue Growth Rate
**Definition:** Monthly and yearly revenue growth percentage  
**Target:** 15% YoY growth  
**Calculation:** ((Current Period - Previous Period) / Previous Period) × 100  

**What Good Looks Like:**
- Consistent month-over-month growth
- Strong performance across all business units
- Growth rate exceeding industry benchmarks

#### Profit Margin
**Definition:** Net profit as percentage of total revenue  
**Target:** 25% net profit margin  
**Calculation:** (Net Profit / Total Revenue) × 100  

**Key Drivers:**
- Project efficiency and delivery
- Cost management and overhead control
- Premium pricing for high-value services

#### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
**Definition:** Total value a customer generates over their relationship  
**Target:** €150,000 average CLV  
**Calculation:** Average monthly value × Customer lifespan in months  

### Operational KPIs

#### On-Time Delivery Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of projects delivered on or before deadline  
**Target:** 95% on-time delivery  
**Impact:** Direct correlation with customer satisfaction and retention  

#### Team Utilization Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of available time spent on billable work  
**Target:** 75% utilization rate  
**Balance:** High enough for profitability, low enough for innovation time  

#### Customer Satisfaction Score
**Definition:** Average rating from post-project surveys  
**Target:** 4.5/5.0 average satisfaction  
**Frequency:** Measured after each project completion  

## Alerts and Notifications

### Critical Alerts (Red)
- Revenue decline >10% month-over-month
- Profit margin below 15%
- Customer satisfaction below 4.0
- Project delivery delays >2 weeks

### Warning Alerts (Yellow)
- Revenue growth slowing
- Customer churn increasing
- Team utilization below 70%
- Pipeline value declining

### Information Alerts (Blue)
- New customer milestones reached
- Positive market trends identified
- Team achievements and recognition

## Troubleshooting

### Data Issues

#### Missing or Delayed Data
**Possible Causes:**
- API connectivity issues with source systems
- Pipeline processing delays
- Data quality validation failures

**What to Do:**
1. Check data freshness indicators on dashboard
2. Contact data engineering team if delays exceed 24 hours
3. Use previous period data for urgent decisions

#### Inconsistent Numbers
**Possible Causes:**
- Data processing timing differences
- Currency conversion fluctuations
- Different calculation methodologies

**What to Do:**
1. Note discrepancies and timeframes
2. Request clarification from finance team
3. Use official financial reports for critical decisions

### Dashboard Performance

#### Slow Loading
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Check internet connection stability
- Try accessing during off-peak hours
- Contact IT support if issues persist

#### Display Issues
- Update browser to latest version
- Disable browser extensions temporarily
- Try different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Access mobile version if desktop fails
